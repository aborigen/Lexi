## 2. Localized Descriptions

### English (en)
- **Title**: Lexi.AI - Word Connect Puzzle
- **Short Description**: An engaging circular word-linking game with AI-powered hints.
- **Full Description**:
Step into the serene world of Lexi.AI, where words come to life under a bright blue sky!

Lexi.AI is an intuitive word-linking puzzle that challenges your vocabulary and cognitive skills. Connect letters in circular motions to discover hidden combinations and clear levels. 

Feeling stuck? Use our unique **Lexical AI Advisor** to receive clever, cryptic hints that guide you without giving the answer away. Perfect for players who love crosswords, word searches, and brain training.

**Features:**
- ✨ **Circular Interaction**: Intuitive line-drawing mechanics for word formation.
- 🤖 **AI-Powered Advisor**: Smart hints to help you through tough levels.
- ☀️ **Blue Sky Aesthetic**: Relaxing visual design with a sunny atmosphere.
- 🏆 **High Score Sync**: Compete for the top spot on the leaderboard.
- 🌍 **Fully Localized**: Play in English or Russian.
